A café in a former bank — Chef Liz Jackson's grab & heat meals, sandwiches on house-made bread, and soups worth crossing the Whites for.

Call: 603-723-6265
Yes, there's a vault. Birdie's serves lunch out of Gorham's old bank building — grab-and-go from the cooler, and pop-up in-house dining (watch the schedule).

From the cooler & the counter

Grab & Heat

Rotating chef-made meals to take home: Thai green curry, chicken korma, shrimp curry, fettuccine, and feel-good soups from the freezer.

Sandwiches

On house focaccia and sourdough: roasted turkey, ham & fontina, herby chicken salad, chicken shawarma, and a veg wrap — gluten-free wraps available.

Sweet finish

The famed carrot cake, plus cookies and house focaccia to go.

Chef Liz Jackson

Liz Jackson — founder of Libby's Bistro and SAaLT, cooking on Main Street since 1997 with locally grown ingredients from sustainable farms — now brings the same seasonal, worldly cooking to a casual café counter. Birdie's is rated 4.7 stars from 224 reviews. Open Thursday–Sunday, 11:30 AM – 6:00 PM; in-house dining is pop-up style, so check the week's schedule before you come.
